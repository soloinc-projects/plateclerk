PlateClerk

Privacy policy

PlateClerk, by 8086 LLC · Effective October 26, 2026

PlateClerk collects no data. We, 8086 LLC, receive nothing from the app: no invoices, no photos, no prices, no dishes, no usage figures, no identifiers.

What stays on your phone

The invoices you photograph or open, their photos and PDFs, the lines you confirm, your prices, your dishes, your restaurant's and suppliers' names, and the layout the app learns for each supplier's invoices. They are kept in the app's own storage on your phone, protected by the phone's operating system. They are read on the phone; nothing is uploaded, and there is no server that could receive them.

What is never stored

Card numbers and bank or remittance lines printed on an invoice are dropped before anything is saved. The app has no account, so it holds no email address, phone number or password. It uses no advertising identifier, no analytics and no tracking, and shares nothing with anyone.

A small event log, on the phone only

The app keeps a short list of its own recent events (for example, "an invoice was confirmed, 12 lines") so a support question can be answered. It holds counts and kinds only, never invoice content or names, and it never leaves the phone unless you copy it from Settings and send it to us yourself.

Purchases

The subscription is sold by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). They handle payment under their own privacy policies; the app learns only whether a subscription is active, and never sees your card or account details.

Age signal

Where the law of your state requires it, the app reads, once, the age range your App Store or Google Play account shares (Apple's Declared Age Range, Google Play's Age Signals). It uses it only for that decision, never stores or sends it, and gates nothing, because nothing in the app is unsuitable for any age.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to photograph invoices, when you choose to. Photos you pick come through the system's photo picker, which shows the app only the photos you choose.

Sharing and export

When you share the weekly note or export everything, the file goes where you send it through the phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent otherwise.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete. To remove everything, open Settings › Delete everything: it removes all invoices, photos, prices and dishes from the phone. Deleting the app does the same. Neither cancels a subscription; cancel that in your App Store or Google Play account.

Children

PlateClerk is a business tool for restaurant owners and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with its date. Questions: support@8086.llc, 8086 LLC.
