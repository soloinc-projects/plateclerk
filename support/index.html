Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Everything stays on your phone.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. Photographing, reading, confirming, dishes, the weekly note and export all work offline. Only buying or restoring the subscription needs a connection.

Why is it asking me about a line?

It asks when a pack price moved more than 25%, when a pack size changed, or when it hasn't seen an item before. Answer "Yes, it's right" or correct it. Nothing posts until every question is answered.

It couldn't read my invoice.

Try again in good light with the whole page in view and flat. You can also choose a clearer photo, open the PDF your supplier emailed, or type the lines by hand. It never posts a line it guessed.

What do plate cost, food cost and "what's left" mean?

Plate cost is what the ingredients of one plate cost at this week's prices. Food cost is plate cost as a share of the menu price. What's left is the menu price minus the plate cost; it is not profit, because rent, staff and other costs are not in it.

Which dishes does the weekly note name?

Each dish whose plate cost moved 3% or more, or whose share left moved 2 points or more, with the invoice line that moved it. If nothing moved that much, it says so and names the largest move.

Will it tell me what to charge?

No. It reports what changed. Your menu prices are your decision.

How do I get my data out, or delete it?

Settings › Export everything makes a CSV file with every price, dish and confirmed line, plus the invoice photos. Settings › Delete everything removes it all from the phone.

How does the subscription work?

The app starts with a 14-day free trial. Then it is $29.99 a month or $249.99 a year (U.S. prices). There is no per-invoice charge and no invoice limit. If you don't subscribe, everything you confirmed stays readable. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions; deleting the app does not cancel it.

I reinstalled or changed phones.

Tap Restore purchases in Settings to bring back your subscription. Your invoices and dishes live only on the old phone; export them there first and keep the file.
