Who it's for

PlateClerk is for the owner of one independent restaurant who buys from a supplier on invoices and wants to know what this week's prices did to the menu. It is not for restaurant groups with a finance office, for anyone who needs inventory counts, purchase orders, bill pay or a till link, or for kitchens outside the United States.

It reports what changed. It never tells you what to charge.
