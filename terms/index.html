What PlateClerk does

PlateClerk reads supplier invoices on your phone, asks you to confirm what it read, and works out what your dishes cost from the prices you confirmed. Its figures are only as right as the invoices and the answers you give it. It reports what changed; it does not advise on menu prices, and it is not your system of record: keep your invoices as you do today.
